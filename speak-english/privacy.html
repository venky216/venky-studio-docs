Privacy Policy for English Speaking Practice (Speak English)

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how the English Speaking Practice mobile app, shown on your phone as "Speak English" ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

Your voice stays on your phone

The app uses your microphone only while you are practising, after you tap the microphone button. Speech recognition runs entirely on your device using a speech model built into the app. Your recordings and the text recognised from them are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Your latest recording is kept temporarily on your device so you can play it back ("My voice"), and is overwritten by later recordings or removed when the app's temporary files are cleared. You can turn off the microphone permission at any time in your phone's settings; listening lessons still work without it.

Information stored on your device

Your practice progress (lessons finished, clarity scores, minutes spoken, streak), your learning goal and your settings (model voice, speed, reminder time, theme) are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information, and we do not require an account. You can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Model voice

Example sentences are read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech engine. The text is processed on your device by that engine; the app does not send it to us.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules one notification a day on your device. No information is sent to us. Turn it off at any time in the app or in your phone's notification settings.

Feedback

If you choose "Send feedback", your email app opens with a draft addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a small banner on lesson lists, an occasional full-screen ad after you finish a practice, and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch to open a Pro lesson for a short time. Ads are never shown while you are speaking, and Pro removes all ads. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

Your voice recordings are never shared with advertisers. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Me → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Pro purchases and subscriptions

Pro is offered as an auto-renewing subscription (with an optional free trial) or as a one-time purchase. Payments, free trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive or store your payment details. The app asks Google Play only whether your account currently owns Pro. See Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and our Terms of Use.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
