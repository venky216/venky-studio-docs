Privacy Policy for Waterblu: Drink Water Reminder

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how the Waterblu: Drink Water Reminder mobile app ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The drinks you log, your daily goal, the profile you use to work it out (sex, body weight, activity level, climate, whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding, and any fluid limit your doctor has set, each only if you choose to say so), your wake-up and bedtime, streaks and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information, and we do not require an account. You can delete it at any time with You → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app.

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account, and on an iPhone, iCloud Backup may include it in your iCloud account. We cannot see those backups.

Health information

The daily goal is a general suggestion based on common guidance, not medical advice. The app does not read data from other health apps and does not share your logs with anyone.

Reminders and the home-screen widget

If you turn on drink reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device between the wake-up and bedtime you set. If you add the home-screen widget, today's amount is saved where the widget can show it. Neither sends information to us. You can turn reminders off in You → Drink reminders or in your phone's notification settings.

Sending feedback

If you choose "Send feedback", your email app opens with a draft addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Advertising

The app shows an ad only when you choose to: you can watch a short video ad provided by Google AdMob to try a locked friend for a day. There are no banners and no ads that appear on their own, and logging a drink never shows an ad. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

Your drink log, goal and profile are never shared with Google or advertisers.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in You → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Waterblu Pro (a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) is processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy, and Apple's in Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links

The app may open links (for example, this policy or the store page) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
