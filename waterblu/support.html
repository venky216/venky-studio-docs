Common questions

How is my daily goal worked out?

It starts from the European Food Safety Authority's daily water intake for adults (2 litres for women, 2.5 litres for men), less the part that usually comes from food, then adjusts a little for your weight, activity, climate, pregnancy or breastfeeding. Open You → Daily goal to see every step, or set your own number. If a doctor has given you a fluid limit, choose that option and Waterblu uses your doctor's number.

I forgot to log a drink yesterday.

Open the Journal, go back to that day with the arrows or the calendar (up to 30 days), and tap Add a missed drink.

Reminders don't arrive.

Check You → Drink reminders is on and that notifications are allowed for Waterblu in your phone's settings. Smart reminders stay quiet while you are ahead of your pace and stop once you reach your goal. Battery savers can also delay notifications.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Google account or Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On Android: Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. There is no account and we have no servers that receive your logs. See the privacy policy and terms of use.

Waterblu is a wellness app, not medical advice. If you have a heart, kidney or other condition, ask your doctor how much to drink.
