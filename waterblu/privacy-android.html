Privacy Policy for Waterblu: Drink Water Reminder

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how the Waterblu: Drink Water Reminder mobile app ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The drinks you log, your daily goal, the profile you use to work it out (sex, body weight, activity level, climate, whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding, and any fluid limit your doctor has set, each only if you choose to say so), your wake-up and bedtime, your quick drinks and any drinks you add yourself, caffeine and sugar limits, reminder times and wording, streaks, ice cubes, challenges, badges, your year calendar and Year in review, and your settings (friend, theme, app icon, sounds) are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information (only the anonymous usage statistics described below are sent), and we do not require an account. You can delete it at any time with You → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app.

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account, and on an iPhone, iCloud Backup may include it in your iCloud account. We cannot see those backups.

Backup files

With You → Backup and export you can save or send a backup file, or a table of your drinks (.csv). The app makes the file on your device and hands it to the place you choose (your files, a cloud drive, mail or chat). We never receive it. A backup file contains your log, your profile and your settings, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Health information

The daily goal is a general suggestion based on common guidance, not medical advice. The app does not read data from other health apps and does not share your logs or profile with anyone, including the usage statistics described below.

Reminders, widgets and Live Activity

If you turn on drink reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device between the wake-up and bedtime you set. The buttons on a reminder (such as "+ drink" or "in 30 min") log a drink or move the reminder on your device.

If you add a Waterblu widget (on Android: home-screen widgets; on iPhone: Home Screen, Lock Screen and Control Center widgets) or use the Live Activity on iPhone, today's amount, your goal, your chosen friend and your quick drinks are saved in storage on your device that the widget can read (on iPhone, a shared app group that only Waterblu and its widgets can use). Drinks you log from a widget are saved the same way. The Live Activity is updated on your device only, not through a server. None of this sends information to us. Lock Screen widgets and the Live Activity can be seen by anyone who looks at your locked phone; remove them if you prefer.

You can turn reminders off in You → Drink reminders or in your phone's notification settings, and remove widgets from your Home Screen or Lock Screen at any time.

Watches

If you install Waterblu on a Wear OS watch or an Apple Watch, the phone app sends it what the watch shows: today's amount and goal, your chosen friend and its pictures, your quick drinks and the list of drinks, the last seven days, and (with Pro) this month. Drinks you log or take back on the watch are sent to the phone app and kept on the watch until the phone has them. On Wear OS this goes through Google Play services, over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, and through Google's servers when your phone and watch are not near each other; on Apple Watch it goes through Apple's Watch Connectivity between your iPhone and your watch. Google and Apple handle this under their own policies. None of it is sent to us. A Wear OS watch can also be used without the phone app: it then keeps a goal, a glass size and the drinks you log on the watch only.

Sharing

You can share a report, a Year in review card or a short message about the app. The app makes the picture or text on your device and opens your phone's share sheet; nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it.

Usage statistics and crash reports

To learn which parts of the app people use and to fix problems, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. They receive anonymous information such as which screens are opened, the kind of action taken (for example "a drink was logged from the quick row", "the daily goal was reached", "the Pro page was opened"), the app version, device model and operating system, an app-instance identifier created by Firebase, approximate country (from the IP address), and crash logs. They never receive your amounts, goal, weight, sex, pregnancy or breastfeeding status, fluid limit, the names of your own drinks, or anything else from your profile or logs, and they do not use your advertising ID (on Android the advertising ID is turned off for Firebase; on iPhone the app never asks for permission to track, so the advertising identifier is not available). On iPhone, Firebase may use the identifier Apple gives each app maker (the "identifier for vendor") to count app installs. This data is not used for advertising. You can turn it off at any time in You → Share usage stats. Google keeps this data according to its own policies (Firebase privacy).

Sending feedback

If you choose "Send feedback", your email app opens with a draft addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Advertising

On iPhone

The iPhone app shows no ads. The ad software is not started, the app does not use your advertising identifier, and it never asks for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

On Android

The Android app shows an ad only when you choose to: you can watch a short video ad provided by Google AdMob to try a locked friend for a day. There are no banners and no ads that appear on their own, logging a drink never shows an ad, and Waterblu Pro users never see ads. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

Your drink log, goal and profile are never shared with Google or advertisers.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in You → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

Waterblu Pro (a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) is processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy, and Apple's in Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links

The app may open links (for example, this policy or the store page) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
