Privacy Policy for Dictionary: Vocabulary Builder

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how the Dictionary: Vocabulary Builder mobile app ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

The dictionary works offline

All word lookups, searches and tools run on your device. The words you search for are never sent to us or to anyone else. The dictionary data is downloaded once from Google Play when you install the app.

Information stored on your device

Your lookup history, the words you learn and review, your lessons, word lists, streaks, scores and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information, and we do not require an account. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Text from other apps

If you select a word in another app and choose "Define", or share text to the app, the app receives that text only to look it up on your device. It is added to your on-device history like any other lookup and is not sent anywhere.

Pronunciation

Words and examples are read aloud either by your phone's own text-to-speech engine or, if you choose to download them, by the app's natural voices. The natural voices are downloaded from Google Play and make speech entirely on your device: the words being read are never sent anywhere. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Word of the day notifications

If you turn on the daily word, the app schedules notifications on your device. No information is sent to us, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Feedback

If you choose "Send feedback", your email app opens with a draft addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The optional "Dictionary Pro" (a monthly or yearly subscription, the yearly one with a free trial, or a one-time lifetime purchase) is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions. Google Play's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Dictionary content

Definitions and other content come from open, community-written sources (Wiktionary, Open English WordNet, Tatoeba and others), credited in the app under Settings → Sources & credits. Like any large dictionary, it describes how words are used, including words some people find offensive; such words are labelled.

Links

The app may open links (for example, this policy, the credits or the store page) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
