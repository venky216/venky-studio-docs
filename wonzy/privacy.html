Privacy Policy for Wonzy: General Knowledge

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how the Wonzy: General Knowledge mobile app ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

No account, no servers

The app does not require an account, and we do not run servers that receive information about you. Lessons, quizzes, search and everything else run on your device.

Information stored on your device

Your name (if you enter one), your progress through journeys, quiz answers, saved cards, Recall reviews, streaks, badges and settings are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time with Me → Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Photos and narration

Journey photos and the recorded narration for Listen mode are downloaded from Google Play as part of the app when you need them. No personal information is sent to us when they download. Narration is pre-recorded audio played on your device. The app does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

Reminders and the home-screen widget

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules notifications on your device. If you add the "On this day" widget, it shows content that is already in the app. No information is sent to us, and you can turn reminders off at any time in the app's settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Sharing

When you share a certificate or a challenge card, the app opens your phone's share sheet with an image and text. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share it with, and we never receive it.

Feedback

If you choose "Send feedback", your email app opens with a draft addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Me → Settings → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The optional one-time "Wonzy Pro" purchase (which removes ads and unlocks extra features such as Listen mode, the Time Machine and jumping ahead) is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Content and credits

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons contributors and "On this day" anniversaries from Wikipedia, under their open licences; they are credited in the app under Photo credits. History journeys cover real events, including wars, and the art journey shows famous works of art, some of which include classical nudity.

Links

The app may open links (for example, this policy, photo sources, Wikipedia or the store page) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
