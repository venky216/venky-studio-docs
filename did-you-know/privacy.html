Privacy Policy for Did You Know? Scroll Facts

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how the Did You Know? Scroll Facts mobile app ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The topics you pick, the facts you like and save, what your feed has learned about your interests, your quiz answers, reading streak, time spent and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information, and we do not require an account. You can delete it at any time in You → Settings → Clear likes, saves & stats, or by uninstalling the app.

Photos and "Tell me more"

All facts are stored in the app and work offline. When you are online and photos are turned on, the app loads freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons and, when you tap "Tell me more", the opening paragraph of the related Wikipedia article. These requests go directly from your device to Wikimedia's servers, which receive your IP address and the name of the article or image, as with any website. See the Wikimedia privacy policy: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy. You can turn photos off in Settings.

Fact of the day notifications

If you turn on the daily fact, the app schedules notifications on your device. No information is sent to us, and you can turn them off at any time in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Feedback

If you choose "Send feedback", your email app opens with a draft addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (never on top of the fact feed). To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The optional "Pro" purchase (removes ads) is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Content

Most facts come from Wikipedia's community-written "Did you know…?" section (CC BY-SA 4.0) and are credited on every card. The app is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
