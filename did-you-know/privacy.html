Privacy Policy for Did You Know? Scroll Facts

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how the Did You Know? Scroll Facts mobile app ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The topics you pick, the facts you like and save, what your feed has learned about your interests, your quiz answers, reading streak, time spent and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information, and we do not require an account. You can delete it at any time in You → Settings → Clear likes, saves & stats, or by uninstalling the app.

Photos and stories

All facts, and the stories behind most of them, are stored in the app and work offline. When you are online and photos are turned on, the app loads freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons, and for stories that aren't stored in the app, the opening of the related Wikipedia article. These requests go directly from your device to Wikimedia's servers, which receive your IP address and the name of the article or image, as with any website. See the Wikimedia privacy policy: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy. You can turn photos off in Settings.

Listen

"Listen" reads facts and stories aloud. On Android you can download an optional natural voice (about 100 MB, delivered by Google Play the same way as app updates). Once downloaded, the speech is made entirely on your device; no text or audio leaves your phone. Otherwise, and on iPhone, your phone's own text-to-speech engine reads; it processes the text on your device and the app does not send it to us. Speech made by the natural voice is kept briefly in the app's cache so a fact you hear again starts at once.

Fact on lock screen

If you turn on "Fact on lock screen", the app sets your lock screen wallpaper to a fact card from time to time (it never changes your home screen). To draw Pro photo cards, it downloads the fact's photo and its credit from Wikimedia Commons, as described above. Turning the feature off gives your lock screen your home screen wallpaper again.

Daily fact notifications

If you turn on daily facts, the app schedules up to three notifications a day on your device. They are created on your phone; no information is sent to us. Turn them off at any time in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

Feedback

If you choose "Send feedback", your email app opens with a draft addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob (a small banner, clearly labelled "Sponsored" cards between facts and occasional full-screen ads after quizzes; Pro removes all ads). To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Pro purchases and subscriptions

Pro is offered as an auto-renewing subscription (with an optional free trial) or as a one-time purchase. Payments, free trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled entirely by Google Play; we do not receive or store your payment details. The app asks Google Play only whether your account currently owns Pro. Google Play's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. See also our Terms of Use.

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Content

Most facts come from Wikipedia's community-written "Did you know…?" section (CC BY-SA 4.0) and are credited on every card. The app is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation.
