Privacy Policy for Vocabulary: Learn English

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how the Vocabulary: Learn English mobile app ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your first name (if you enter one), learned words, review schedule, favourites, streaks, badges and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information, and we do not require an account. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app.

Pronunciation (text-to-speech)

When you tap a speaker button, the app asks your phone's built-in text-to-speech service to read the word aloud. We do not receive or record any audio. The speech service is provided by your device (for example Google's speech services on Android) under its own privacy policy.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your device. No information is sent to us, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Reminder or in your phone's notification settings.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier,
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app and your interactions with ads,
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and certain US states, the app asks for your consent or choices using Google's consent tools before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices (when available in your region). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more about how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links

The app may open links (for example, this policy or the store page) in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
