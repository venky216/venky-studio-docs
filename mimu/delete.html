Delete your Mimu account and data

This page is for people who use Mimu: Baby Tracker & Feeding by Venky Studio. Mimu only has an account for you if you turned on Share with family or joined a family. If you never did, nothing about you is stored outside your phone, and deleting the app removes everything.

Ask us to delete it (no app needed)

Write to venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Mimu account".

  • If you added a sign-in (Apple, Google or email), write from that email address.
  • If you did not add a sign-in, tell us the name you used in the family and, if you still have it, an invite code from that family, so we can find the right account.

We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Do it yourself in the app

  • Delete your sign-in: Family → Your sign-in → Delete my sign-in. Your account is deleted at once.
  • Leave a family: Family → Leave this family. Your phone stops sharing.

What is deleted

  • Your account: its identifier and, if you added a sign-in, your email address.
  • Your membership of the family: your name, your role and the date you joined.
  • The family's shared log in the cloud, when the last member leaves or is deleted. While other members remain, the shared log stays for them, including entries you made.

What is not deleted, and what is kept

  • The log on your own phone and on the phones of other family members. We cannot reach those. On your phone, use You → Your data → Delete everything, or delete the app.
  • Backup files, reports and messages you saved or sent yourself.
  • Purchases: Google Play or the App Store keeps the record of a purchase. Cancel a subscription in the store.

We keep nothing else after deletion. Google may hold deleted data in its backups for a limited time before it is removed, as its own terms describe.

Delete some data without deleting the account

Any entry you delete in the app is removed from the cloud copy and from every family phone. A parent can remove a member in Family, and that person stops receiving the log.

More detail is in the Privacy Policy.
