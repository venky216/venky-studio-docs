Privacy Policy for Mimu: Baby Tracker & Feeding

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how the Mimu mobile app ("the app"), published by Venky Studio ("we"), handles information. Mimu is for parents and carers; it holds information about your baby that you choose to write down. If you have questions, contact us at venkyroyals.contact@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Everything you log stays on your phone (and on your watch, if you use Mimu there).
  • Mimu has no accounts and we run no servers that receive your information. We cannot see your log.
  • No ads. No selling of data. No analytics or tracking software.

Information stored on your device

Your baby's name, birthday, sex and the look you chose for Mimu; everything you log (feeds, sleep, nappies or diapers, pumping and the milk stash, growth measurements, medicines as you typed them, temperatures, symptoms, foods, teeth, firsts, notes and your own trackers); the vaccine record and appointments you entered; the phone numbers you saved; photos you attached; and your settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive this information and we cannot see it.

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account, and on an iPhone, iCloud Backup may include it in your iCloud account. We cannot see those backups.

Health information

Mimu records what you enter and does arithmetic on it (totals, time since, your baby's own usual pattern). It contains no medical reference data and gives no medical advice. Information about your baby's feeding, sleep, growth, medicines and vaccinations is sensitive: anyone you give a backup file, a report or a summary to can read it.

Reminders, widgets and Live Activity

Reminders are scheduled on your device. If you add a Mimu widget (Android home screen; iPhone Home Screen and Lock Screen) or use the Live Activity on iPhone, a small summary (your baby's name and age, whether they are asleep or feeding and since when, the time of the last feed and nappy, today's counts) and drawings of Mimu are saved in storage on your device that the widget can read. Buttons on a widget log on your device. None of this sends information to us. Lock Screen widgets and the Live Activity can be seen by anyone who can see your locked phone.

Watches

If you install Mimu on a Wear OS watch or an Apple Watch, the phone app sends it the same small summary and drawings, and the watch sends back what you tap (for example "fell asleep" or "wet nappy"). On Wear OS this goes through Google Play services, over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi, and through Google's servers when your phone and watch are not near each other; on Apple Watch it goes through Apple's Watch Connectivity. We do not receive it.

Siri, Shortcuts and speaking a log

On iPhone you can log by voice or from the Shortcuts app (for example "Log wet nappy in Mimu"). Apple handles the voice request under its own privacy policy; the log itself is made on your device. "Say it or type it" uses the microphone button of your own keyboard: your keyboard turns speech into text and Mimu only receives the text. Mimu does not ask for microphone access.

Photos

Photos you attach (for example a page of the health book or a medicine label) are ones you pick yourself. They are copied into the app's storage on your phone and are never uploaded.

Backup files, reports and sending the day

You can save a backup file, export a table (.csv), make a report for the doctor (PDF) or send a summary of the day or a handover as a message. The app makes these on your device and hands them to the place you choose (your files, a cloud drive, mail or chat). We never receive them. A backup file holds your whole log and settings, so keep it somewhere you trust.

Purchases

Mimu Pro (a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) is processed by Google Play or the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google's handling of purchase information is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy, and Apple's in Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Advertising and analytics

Mimu shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics software. It does not use your advertising identifier and never asks to track you.

Contacting us

If you choose "Contact us", your email app opens with a draft addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself, and we only use it to reply to you.

Who may use Mimu

Mimu is for adults (parents and carers). It is not directed at children. The information about your baby is entered by you and stays with you.

Your rights and deleting your data

Everything Mimu holds is on your device, visible and editable in the app. You → Your data lets you save or send a copy, and Delete everything removes all of it from the phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it (copies in your own device backups or backup files stay where you put them). Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to delete; if you have a question, write to us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if a future version adds a way to share the log with family. Changes will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date, and any new feature that sends information off your phone will be optional and explained in the app before you turn it on.
