1. The app

Mimu is a notebook for the people who look after a baby: you log feeds, sleep, changes and other things, and the app shows them back to you. All logging is free, and Mimu Pro unlocks extras. We may add, change or improve features over time.

2. Not medical advice

Mimu is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. It shows only what you entered and arithmetic on it (totals, time since, your baby's own usual pattern). It does not know what is normal or safe for any baby, does not check a medicine, a dose or a waiting time, and holds no vaccine schedule: dates, doses and numbers are the ones you typed. A timer or reminder may be late, wrong or missing, for example when the phone is off, silent or in a power-saving mode, so never rely on Mimu alone for a medicine or a feed. If you are worried about your baby, contact a doctor or your local emergency number.

3. Sharing with family

  • Sharing is optional. When you turn it on, an end-to-end encrypted copy of the log is stored in the cloud as the Privacy Policy describes. Only your family's phones hold the key, so if every phone holding it is lost, the cloud copy cannot be opened by anyone, including us. Keep a backup file if that matters to you.
  • You decide who joins. Anyone who has a code you created can join with the role it carries until it runs out, so send codes only to people you trust. Parents can remove members.
  • Everyone who can log can add, change and delete entries for the whole family. When two people change the same entry, the later change is kept.
  • Keep your own backup (You → Your data) if the log matters to you. We work hard to keep sharing reliable but do not promise that it is always available or free of errors, and we may limit or end it, with notice in the app where we reasonably can.
  • You are responsible for having the right to share information about the child with the people you invite.
